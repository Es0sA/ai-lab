AI, GenAI & Agentic AI Landscape
Understand the full arc of AI evolution from rule-based systems to agentic AI. Learn where Generative AI and Agentic AI fit in the broader landscape, and how each era differs architecturally. Also covers foundational Ethical AI principles.

Learning Objectives

  • Map the 5 eras of AI evolution and explain each transition
  • Distinguish between Classical ML, Deep Learning, Generative AI, and Agentic systems
  • Identify which architecture fits a given business problem
  • Understand how bias enters AI systems and why responsible AI matters
